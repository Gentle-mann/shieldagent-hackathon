# Japanese Labor Law Reference for Migrant Workers
# 日本労働法 外国人労働者向けリファレンス

## 1. Minimum Wage (最低賃金) - Minimum Wage Act (最低賃金法)

### National and Prefectural Minimum Wages (2025-2026)

The minimum wage in Japan is set per prefecture. Employers must pay at least the prefectural minimum wage.

Key prefectural minimum wages (as of October 2025):
- Tokyo (東京): 1,163 yen/hour
- Kanagawa (神奈川): 1,162 yen/hour
- Osaka (大阪): 1,114 yen/hour
- Aichi (愛知): 1,077 yen/hour
- Saitama (埼玉): 1,078 yen/hour
- Chiba (千葉): 1,076 yen/hour
- Hokkaido (北海道): 1,010 yen/hour
- Fukuoka (福岡): 992 yen/hour
- Hiroshima (広島): 1,020 yen/hour
- National weighted average: 1,055 yen/hour

### Violation: Article 4 of the Minimum Wage Act
Paying below the minimum wage is illegal. The employer must pay the difference retroactively. Penalties: fine up to 500,000 yen.

## 2. Working Hours (労働時間) - Labor Standards Act (労働基準法)

### Article 32: Standard Working Hours
- Maximum 8 hours per day
- Maximum 40 hours per week
- Exception: Workplaces with fewer than 10 employees in specified industries may work up to 44 hours/week

### Article 36: Overtime Agreement (36協定 / サブロク協定)
- Overtime work requires a written agreement between employer and worker representative (36 Agreement)
- The agreement must be filed with the Labor Standards Inspection Office
- Without a valid 36 Agreement, ANY overtime is illegal

### Overtime Limits (after 36 Agreement):
- Normal limit: 45 hours/month, 360 hours/year
- Special provisions limit: 100 hours/month (single month), 720 hours/year
- Average over 2-6 months must not exceed 80 hours/month
- Monthly overtime exceeding 45 hours is allowed max 6 times per year

### Article 37: Overtime Pay Rates
- Regular overtime (beyond 8 hours/day or 40 hours/week): 125% of normal wage (1.25x)
- Work on statutory holidays (法定休日): 135% of normal wage (1.35x)
- Late night work (10 PM to 5 AM / 22:00-05:00): additional 25% premium
- Overtime + late night: 150% of normal wage (1.50x)
- Holiday + late night: 160% of normal wage (1.60x)
- Overtime exceeding 60 hours/month: 150% of normal wage (1.50x)

## 3. Rest Periods and Days Off (休憩・休日)

### Article 34: Rest Periods
- Work 6-8 hours: at least 45 minutes rest
- Work over 8 hours: at least 60 minutes (1 hour) rest
- Rest must be given during working hours, not at start or end
- Rest must be given simultaneously to all workers (with some exceptions)

### Article 35: Days Off
- At least 1 day off per week, OR 4 days off per 4-week period
- This is the legal minimum — many contracts provide more

## 4. Wage Payment Rules (賃金支払い) - Labor Standards Act

### Article 24: Five Principles of Wage Payment
1. **Currency payment**: Wages must be paid in currency (cash or bank transfer), not in kind
2. **Direct payment**: Wages must be paid directly to the worker, not through agents or intermediaries
3. **Full payment**: The full amount must be paid. Deductions are only allowed for:
   - Legally mandated items (income tax, social insurance premiums)
   - Items agreed upon in a written labor-management agreement (労使協定)
4. **Monthly payment**: At least once per month
5. **Fixed date payment**: On a predetermined date

### Illegal Deductions
The following deductions are ILLEGAL without a proper labor-management agreement:
- Dormitory/housing fees beyond actual cost
- Tool or uniform fees
- Penalty deductions exceeding limits (max 50% of daily wage per incident, max 10% of total monthly wage)
- "Deposit" or "guarantee money" deductions
- Recruitment/placement fees charged to the worker
- Forced savings managed by the employer (Article 18)

### Article 18: Prohibition of Forced Savings
Employers cannot require workers to save money with the employer or a designated third party. This is absolutely prohibited, with no exceptions.

## 5. Technical Intern Training Program (技能実習制度 / TITP)

### Legal Framework: Technical Intern Training Act (技能実習法)

### Key Protections for TITP Trainees:

#### Article 46: Prohibition on restricting trainees' private life
- Employers cannot confiscate passports or residence cards
- Employers cannot restrict trainees' communication (phone, mail, internet)
- Employers cannot restrict trainees' movement outside work hours

#### Article 47: Prohibition of improper acts
- Human rights violations against trainees
- Forced labor
- Restricting freedom of movement
- Threats of deportation to prevent complaints

#### Article 48: Passport and Residence Card
- Employers MUST NOT confiscate or keep passports
- Employers MUST NOT confiscate or keep residence cards (在留カード)
- Keeping these documents "for safekeeping" is ILLEGAL regardless of the reason given
- Violation: Criminal penalty possible

#### Contract Requirements for TITP:
- Must specify: job duties, working hours, wages, housing, insurance
- Contract must be explained in the trainee's native language
- A copy must be provided to the trainee in their language
- Maximum duration: 3 years (Technical Intern Training No. 1 + No. 2) or 5 years (with No. 3)

#### Job Transfer Rules (TITP):
- Job transfers between employers are generally NOT allowed under TITP
- Exceptions: employer bankruptcy, abuse/violence, natural disaster, employer closure
- The supervising organization (監理団体) must assist with transfers in these cases

### OTIT (Organization for Technical Intern Training / 外国人技能実習機構):
- Consultation hotline: 0120-250-168
- Available in: Japanese, English, Chinese, Vietnamese, Indonesian, Filipino, Thai, Cambodian, Myanmar
- Hours: Monday-Friday, 11:00-19:00

## 6. Specified Skilled Worker (特定技能 / SSW)

### SSW-1 (Specified Skilled Worker No. 1):
- Maximum stay: 5 years total
- Can change employers within the same industry sector
- No family members allowed
- 14 designated industry sectors including: nursing care, building cleaning, agriculture, food service, construction, shipbuilding, automotive repair, aviation, accommodation, agriculture, fishery, food manufacturing, restaurant, material processing

### SSW-2 (Specified Skilled Worker No. 2):
- No limit on duration of stay (renewable indefinitely)
- Can bring family members (spouse and children)
- Currently available in: construction, shipbuilding (expanding to all 14 sectors)

### Key Rights for SSW Workers:
- Same labor protections as Japanese workers
- Right to change employers within the same sector
- Employer must enroll worker in all social insurance programs
- Employer must provide support plan including: orientation, daily life support, complaint handling
- The Registered Support Organization (登録支援機関) must provide native-language support

## 7. Social Insurance (社会保険)

### Mandatory Enrollment
Employers MUST enroll foreign workers in:

1. **Health Insurance (健康保険)**: Covers 70% of medical costs. Employer pays approximately 50%.
2. **Employees' Pension Insurance (厚生年金保険)**: Retirement pension. Foreign workers who leave Japan can claim a Lump-Sum Withdrawal Payment (脱退一時金).
3. **Employment Insurance (雇用保険)**: Unemployment benefits. Required for workers working 20+ hours/week.
4. **Workers' Accident Compensation Insurance (労災保険)**: 100% employer-paid. Covers workplace injuries and commuting accidents. ALL workers including part-time and TITP trainees are covered.

### Enrollment Deadlines
- Health insurance and pension: Within 5 days of employment start
- Employment insurance: By the end of the month following the month of employment start
- Workers' accident insurance: No enrollment per worker (employer covers all workers automatically)

### Violation
Failure to enroll workers in mandatory insurance is illegal. Workers can report to the Japan Pension Service (日本年金機構) or Hello Work (ハローワーク).

## 8. Annual Paid Leave (年次有給休暇)

### Article 39: Paid Leave Entitlement
After 6 months of continuous employment with 80%+ attendance:
- 6 months: 10 days
- 1.5 years: 11 days
- 2.5 years: 12 days
- 3.5 years: 14 days
- 4.5 years: 16 days
- 5.5 years: 18 days
- 6.5+ years: 20 days (maximum)

This applies equally to TITP trainees, SSW workers, and all foreign workers.

### Mandatory 5-Day Usage (2019 reform)
Employers must ensure workers take at least 5 paid leave days per year (for workers with 10+ days entitlement).

## 9. Dismissal Protection (解雇)

### Article 20: Notice of Dismissal
- Employer must give at least 30 days' advance notice of dismissal
- OR pay 30 days' average wages in lieu of notice
- Immediate dismissal requires approval from the Labor Standards Inspection Office

### Article 16: Abuse of Right to Dismiss
Dismissal that lacks objectively reasonable grounds and is not considered appropriate in general societal terms is invalid as an abuse of rights.

### Special Protection
- Workers on maternity/paternity leave cannot be dismissed
- Workers on leave for work-related injury/illness cannot be dismissed during the leave + 30 days after
- Dismissal for filing complaints about labor violations is prohibited (whistleblower protection)

## 10. Harassment Prevention (ハラスメント防止)

### Power Harassment Prevention (パワハラ防止 - 2020 law)
Employers must take measures to prevent:
- Physical attacks
- Mental attacks (threats, insults, excessive criticism)
- Social isolation (ignoring, excluding from activities)
- Excessive demands (clearly unnecessary or impossible tasks)
- Underutilization (assigning degrading tasks well below capability)
- Invasion of privacy

### Sexual Harassment Prevention
Employers must establish complaint mechanisms and take corrective action.

### Consultation Resources
- Labor Bureau (労働局): Each prefecture has one
- General Labor Consultation Corner (総合労働相談コーナー): Free, multilingual
- Phone: 0570-064-110

## 11. New "Ikusei Shuro" System (育成就労制度) - Starting 2027

### Overview
Japan is replacing TITP with the new "Ikusei Shuro" (Training Employment) system:
- Planned launch: April 2027
- Key change: Workers CAN transfer employers after 1-2 years (unlike TITP's restrictions)
- Duration: 3 years (with pathway to SSW-1)
- Same 14 industry sectors as SSW

### Transition Period
- Current TITP trainees will be gradually transitioned
- New applications under TITP will end
- Workers currently in TITP retain their existing rights
